# Code of conduct

Skye Meta's open-source projects are for everyone who wants to build with them. We expect the same from everyone who takes part, whether in issues, pull requests, discussions or email.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept that people have different levels of experience, and help where you can.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, including promotion that has nothing to do with the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to support@skyemeta.com. We review every report and keep the reporter's identity private. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who break these rules.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
